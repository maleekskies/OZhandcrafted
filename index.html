Our Craft

A story stitched by hand.

OZ Handmade Shoe started with a simple desire to create something with my own hands that people could be proud to wear.

What started as a skill gradually became a brand, and along the way, I discovered that building a business is about much more than making beautiful shoes. It is about patience, consistency, learning, sacrifice, and choosing to keep going even when things don't go as planned.

There have been days when materials were unavailable, machines stopped working, and even injuries came from the process of creating. There were moments when the work was exhausting, especially while balancing my regular 9–5 job with making shoes at night. But every challenge taught me something and pushed me to become better at what I do.

Behind every pair of OZ shoes is more than leather, suede, skin, palm, a sole, and stitches. There is time, creativity, attention to detail, and a piece of my journey.

I have watched OZ grow from an idea into a brand that has reached customers beyond Nigeria, including the UK, US and Côte d'Ivoire. Every order, referral, review, and returning customer reminds me why I started.

My goal has never been just to make shoes. I want OZ Handmade Shoe to be a brand people trust for quality, creativity, comfort, and craftsmanship.

I am still learning. I am still growing. And I am still creating.

This is my journey with OZ Handmade Shoe and I believe the best chapters are still being written.

— Yahya Ruqayyah, Founder
