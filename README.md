# Behind-the-scenes video

This powers the "Watch how it's made" section on the homepage. Right now it
shows a "Video coming soon" placeholder. Drop your footage in here and it
will appear automatically — no code changes needed.

## Files to add

| Filename              | What it is                                    |
|------------------------|------------------------------------------------|
| how-its-made.mp4       | The video itself                               |
| poster.jpg             | A thumbnail image shown before the video plays |

## Recommended video specs

- Format: **MP4** (H.264 video codec, AAC audio) — plays everywhere without extra setup.
- Length: **60–120 seconds** is usually the sweet spot for a "process" video — long enough to show the craft, short enough that people actually watch it.
- Resolution: **1080p** is plenty; no need for 4K, it just makes the file heavier without a visible difference on most screens.
- File size: try to keep it **under ~30–50MB**. If your raw phone footage is much bigger than that, send it to me and I'll compress it before adding it in — quality holds up fine at web bitrates.

## Poster image

`poster.jpg` is the still frame shown before someone hits play (like a video cover photo). Any clear, well-lit frame from the video works — roughly 1280×720px (16:9) is ideal.

## Alternative: Instagram/YouTube

If this video already lives on Instagram or YouTube, that's actually the lighter option — no file to host at all, it streams from there. Just share the link and I'll swap the section to an embed instead of a local file.
