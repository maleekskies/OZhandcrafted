Help

Ordering, shipping & care.

Ordering

How do I place an order?

Pick a pair from the Collection, choose your colour and size on the product page, then either send it straight to WhatsApp or add it to your cart to order several pairs at once. Everything gets confirmed with you directly on WhatsApp before we start making.

Is this a real checkout / do I pay on this site?

No — the cart and order forms just put together a clear summary of what you want and send it to us on WhatsApp. Payment, final pricing and delivery are all confirmed directly with you there.

How long does production take?

Every pair is handmade to order, and production typically takes 5–7 working days before it's ready for delivery. Express/urgent orders may be possible for an extra charge — just ask on WhatsApp.

Do you do wholesale?

Yes — boutiques, retailers and stockists get wholesale pricing on bulk orders. Note that one or two pairs doesn't qualify as wholesale. Reach out on WhatsApp to discuss quantities and pricing.

Shipping & Delivery

Where do you deliver?

We're based in Abuja and deliver nationwide within Nigeria — distance is not a barrier. Within Abuja, delivery is via dispatch rider or park driver. Outside Abuja, we ship via park/logistics service to your nearest terminal.

Do you ship outside Nigeria?

Yes — OZ has shipped to customers in the UK, US and Côte d'Ivoire. International shipping cost and timeline are quoted individually based on your location, so please confirm on WhatsApp before ordering.

When are deliveries made?

Deliveries are mainly done on weekends. Urgent/same-day deliveries may attract extra charges — ask on WhatsApp if you need something sooner.

How do I pay?

Payment details are shared directly on WhatsApp once your order is confirmed. Please note: we don't offer payment on delivery — payment confirmation is required before your order ships.

Sizing & Care

How do I know my size?

Check our Size Guide for how to measure your foot at home. If you're still unsure, choose "not sure yet" at checkout and we'll help confirm your size on WhatsApp before production starts. Please note sizes above EU 45 may attract an extra charge.

What if I ordered the wrong size?

Because every pair is handmade to the size you provide, errors from an incorrect size or fitting submitted by the customer can't be corrected or refunded after production. That's why we always recommend confirming your size with us on WhatsApp before you order.

How do I care for leather and suede shoes?

Keep leather pairs away from prolonged water exposure and wipe clean with a soft, slightly damp cloth. For suede, use a suede brush to lift dirt rather than water. Store both away from direct sunlight, and let a leather conditioner or polish keep the material supple over time.
